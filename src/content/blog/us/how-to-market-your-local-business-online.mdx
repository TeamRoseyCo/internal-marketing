You want more customers, right? More bookings, more purchases. But how do you get there?

The answer is simple: **online marketing.** It's not just for big brands anymore. Local businesses can win big online too — you just need the right person doing it.

Here are the basics that will help you get noticed, get booked, and get sales. Follow along.

## Why it matters

You might think, *"I'm local. People will find me anyway."*

Here's the truth: if you're not online, you're invisible to anyone who searches for what you do. Your ideal customer is googling for a service like yours **right now** — and if you're not there, they're booking the competitor that is.

## The basics that actually move the needle

### 1. Show up on Google

Most local buying decisions start with a search. Two things matter most:
- **Google Business Profile** — claim it, fill it out, get reviews.
- **Local SEO on your website** — city + service in your titles, headings, and content.

If you nail just these two, you'll start showing up before half your competitors.

### 2. Run paid ads with intent

Paid ads aren't about throwing money at "boost post" buttons. They're about putting your offer in front of the **exact** people typing the buying-intent keywords for your service.

Google Ads + Facebook/Instagram Ads, run with proper targeting and tracking, will fill your calendar faster than anything organic ever will.

### 3. Make every click count

Sending traffic to a slow, ugly, confusing website is lighting money on fire. Your site should:
- Load in under 3 seconds
- Tell visitors exactly what you do, who you do it for, and how to book
- Have a single clear CTA on every page

## The bottom line

You don't need to do *everything*. You need to do these three things consistently — and you need to do them right.
