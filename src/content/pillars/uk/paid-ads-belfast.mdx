## Common Google Ads Mistakes Belfast Businesses Make

Most Belfast businesses waste 30-50% of their Google Ads budget on preventable mistakes. Avoiding these pitfalls dramatically improves ROI without increasing spend.

**1. Targeting Too Broad**
Belfast businesses often waste budget targeting all of UK or even "All Countries" instead of Greater Belfast postcodes. Why show ads to Londoners who'll never become customers? Tight geographic targeting ensures every pound reaches potential Belfast customers within your service area. Even businesses serving all of Northern Ireland benefit from postcode-level analysis identifying which areas convert profitably versus which drain budget.

**2. No Conversion Tracking**
The cardinal sin of Google Ads management—running campaigns without measuring what actually converts. Without conversion tracking, you can't distinguish which keywords drive valuable leads versus which generate curiosity clicks that waste money. Implement form submission tracking, call tracking via unique phone numbers, and CRM integration attributing revenue to specific campaigns. Can't optimize what you can't measure—tracking infrastructure determines campaign profitability.

**3. Sending Ads to Homepage**
Sending all ad traffic to your homepage dilutes conversion rates dramatically. Dedicated landing pages aligned with ad messaging and optimised for single conversion goals convert 5x better than generic homepages. Belfast plumber advertising "emergency leak repair" should send clicks to a landing page specifically about emergency leak repair—not their homepage with navigation to 12 different services. Match landing page to search intent and remove distracting navigation elements.

**4. Ignoring Negative Keywords**
Failing to add negative keywords allows ads to show for irrelevant searches that waste budget. Belfast businesses advertising professional services shouldn't show for "free," "DIY," "courses," or "jobs" searches. Review search term reports weekly, identifying queries that triggered ads but won't ever convert, and add them as negative keywords. Comprehensive negative keyword lists prevent 20-30% of wasted spend.

**5. Set-and-Forget Mentality**
Google Ads is not a "set it and forget it" channel—competition evolves, search behaviour changes, and algorithms update continuously. Campaigns neglected for weeks deteriorate as competitors adjust bids, new competitors enter auctions, and Google's algorithms penalise inactive accounts. Weekly optimization maintaining ad relevance, bidding competitively, and testing improvements separates profitable campaigns from budget drains. Active management compounds improvements monthly; neglect compounds deterioration.
