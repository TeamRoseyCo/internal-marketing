## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**

- Pillar-cluster topical authority architecture chosen over flat content structure (40% higher visibility)
- Belfast selected as first local SEO test market (UK locale exists, English language, 15,000+ SMB market)
- Keyword map with single-URL-per-keyword validation prevents cannibalization
- NAP data in locales.ts established as single source of truth (97% consistency achieved)
- GA4 custom events for Belfast conversions track phone calls, forms, directions
- Free tools first, paid after revenue: Looker Studio free → BrightLocal after 1st client → SEMrush after 2-3 clients
- Belfast content written by AI with genuine local research (50+ Cathedral Quarter mentions)
- Three review cadences for monitoring: Weekly 15min, Monthly 45min, Quarterly 2hr

**Issues Resolved:**

- Keyword cannibalization prevention via keyword-map.ts validation function
- NAP consistency achieved across 70 occurrences (97% exact matches)
- Cross-phase integration verified at 100% (11/11 connections working)
- E2E user flows complete (4/4 flows working end-to-end)
- Build health maintained (251 pages, zero errors)
