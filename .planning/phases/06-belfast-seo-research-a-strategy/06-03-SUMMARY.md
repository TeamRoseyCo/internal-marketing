## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Primary Differentiation: Topical Authority** | Competitors have 10-20 thin pages; Rosey Co will create 25-32 comprehensive pages | 40% higher visibility vs isolated posts (Semrush 2025), demonstrates expertise through depth |
| **Target SMB Market (£500-2000/month)** | VINDICTA targets enterprise (£100M+ revenue), leaving 15,000+ Belfast SMBs underserved | Differentiation through market focus, avoids competing with enterprise agencies |
| **Review Velocity Target: 2-4/month** | Steady velocity matters more than total count for local rankings; recent reviews weighted 2x more | Closes gap with Digital 24's 105 reviews through consistent velocity, not one-time burst |
| **Belfast First, NI-Wide Later** | Establish authority in concentrated market before expanding | Focused resources, clear domination metrics, easier to track success |
| **Pillar Launch Sequence: SEO → Digital → Social → Ads** | SEO highest commercial value, Digital links to others, Social/Ads complement | Leads with strength, establishes breadth, maximizes early conversion potential |
| **2-3 Pages Per Week Publishing Schedule** | Avoid Google spam signals from bulk content launches | Natural content velocity, proper indexing time, sustainable for small team |
| **Start with Free Tools, Upgrade After 2-3 Clients** | SEMrush ($199/mo) pays for itself with one £1,500/mo client | Budget-conscious, proves ROI before investment, practical for startup phase |
| **30-50 Citations Minimum (Phase 11)** | Citations 3x more important for AI search vs traditional (Whitespark 2026) | Future-proofs strategy for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) |

## Key Strategy Highlights

### Competitive Positioning

**vs Digital 24 (105 reviews, 4.96/5):**
- Out-content through pillar-cluster model
- Match/exceed review velocity (2-4/month)
- Differentiate through topical authority depth

**vs VINDICTA (£100M+ revenue, enterprise):**
- Target underserved SMB market
- SMB-focused pricing and content
- Personal, hands-on service vs corporate agency

**vs Outrank (Award-winning):**
- Practical results over industry awards
- Measurable outcomes (traffic, leads, revenue)
- Case studies with specific metrics

**vs SEO NI (NI-focused, 5-star service):**
- Multi-service breadth (4 services vs SEO-only)
- Topical authority content model
- Modern SEO (AI search, E-E-A-T)

**vs GEKKOSHOT (SEO + Social, creative):**
- Technical SEO depth + Google Ads offering
- Strategic + creative (not just creative-first)
- Topical authority demonstration

### Content Architecture

**4 Primary Pillars:**
1. SEO Belfast - 6 clusters (local, small business, technical, link building, cost, how-to)
2. Social Media Marketing Belfast - 5 clusters (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, strategy)
3. Google Ads Belfast - 5 clusters (PPC, cost, search ads, shopping ads, management)
4. Digital Marketing Belfast - 4 clusters (strategy, best agency, lead generation, website traffic)

**Total Content:** 4 pillars (2500-3000 words) + 20 clusters (1000-1500 words) = 24 comprehensive pages

**Differentiation:** 25-32 pages with 2-3x word count vs competitors' 10-20 thin pages

- 25-30 keywords in top 10 (15-20 in top 3)
- 90%+ local pack appearance for service keywords
- +800% Belfast organic traffic vs baseline

**GBP Performance:**
- 50+ reviews with 4.8+ rating
- 4-6 reviews per month velocity
- +500% GBP views vs baseline

**Business Impact:**
- 100+ qualified Belfast leads per month
- 7-10% conversion rate from organic
- £150-200K monthly recurring revenue from Belfast market
