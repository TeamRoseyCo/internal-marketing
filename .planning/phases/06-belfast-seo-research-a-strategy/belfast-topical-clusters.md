## Keyword-to-Cluster Assignment Validation

**Validation rules:**
1. ✅ Each keyword group shares semantic relationship
2. ✅ Mixed intent within cluster (pillar = commercial, clusters = range)
3. ✅ Natural linking opportunities identified
4. ✅ No keyword assigned to multiple clusters with same intent
5. ✅ Cluster size manageable (5-8 cluster pages per pillar)
6. ✅ Aligned with Rosey Co service offerings

**Anti-patterns avoided:**
- ❌ Keywords with identical intent on separate pages (cannibalization risk)
- ❌ Clusters too broad (not cohesive topic)
- ❌ Clusters too narrow (not enough content depth)
- ❌ Orphan keywords not assigned to any cluster
