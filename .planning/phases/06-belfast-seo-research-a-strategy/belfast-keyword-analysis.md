## Opportunity Score Framework

Since we don't have exact search volumes, prioritizing by opportunity factors:

**High Opportunity = High Score:**
- ✅ Aligns with core service (SEO, social, Google Ads, web design)
- ✅ Commercial/transactional intent (not just informational)
- ✅ Manageable competition (competitors have thin content)
- ✅ Belfast-specific (not UK-wide generic)
- ✅ SMB-relevant (our target market)

**Lower Opportunity = Lower Score:**
- ❌ Highly competitive (5+ strong competitors ranking)
- ❌ Purely informational (low conversion potential)
- ❌ Not aligned with services
- ❌ Niche/specialized beyond SMB market

## Validation Strategy (Post-Launch)

Once site launches and ranks:

**Measure via Google Search Console:**
- Which keywords drive impressions (actual search volume)
- Which keywords drive clicks (conversion potential)
- Average position per keyword
- CTR per keyword (identify optimization opportunities)

**Adjust strategy based on:**
- Higher-than-expected volume keywords → Create more cluster content
- Lower-than-expected volume keywords → Deprioritize or bundle
- Unexpected ranking keywords → Double down on those topics
- Competitor movement → React to competitive threats
