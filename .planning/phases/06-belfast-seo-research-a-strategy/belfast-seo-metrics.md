# Belfast SEO Success Metrics & KPIs

**Purpose:** Define measurable success criteria for Belfast SEO campaign to track progress, demonstrate ROI, and guide strategy adjustments.

**Measurement Philosophy:** Track leading indicators (rankings, traffic) and lagging indicators (leads, revenue) to understand both SEO performance and business impact.

#### Conversion Rate from Organic Belfast Traffic

**Metric:** Percentage of Belfast organic visitors who convert to leads

**Why It Matters:** Measures content effectiveness + CTA optimization. Industry benchmark: 2-5% for B2B services.

| Timeframe | Target Conversion Rate | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Month 3 | 3-5% | Baseline with initial content |
| Month 6 | 5-7% | Optimized CTAs and landing pages |
| Month 12 | 7-10% | Refined content and conversion paths |

**Calculation:**
```
Conversion Rate = (Belfast Organic Leads / Belfast Organic Users) × 100

Example:
- 500 Belfast organic users/month
- 25 lead conversions
- Conversion Rate = (25 / 500) × 100 = 5%
```

**Measurement Tool:** GA4 > Exploration (create funnel report)

**Conversion Funnel to Track:**
```
1. Belfast organic visitor lands on site
2. Visits pillar or cluster page
3. Clicks CTA or navigates to contact page
4. Submits form or calls/messages via GBP
5. Becomes qualified lead in CRM
```

**Optimization Levers:**
- Low funnel entry? Improve rankings and meta descriptions
- Drop at page view? Improve content relevance and engagement
- Drop at CTA click? Test CTA copy, placement, design
- Drop at form submit? Simplify form, reduce friction

### Yellow Light (Needs Attention)

**Warning Signs:**
- ⚠️ Rankings declining or stagnant for 2+ months
- ⚠️ Local pack appearance <50% at month 6
- ⚠️ Review velocity <2/month consistently
- ⚠️ Conversion rate <3% at month 6
- ⚠️ High bounce rate (>70%) on pillar pages
- ⚠️ Pages not indexing within 2 weeks

**Action Required:**
- Investigate ranking drops (algorithm update? competitor action?)
- Audit GBP optimization and citation consistency
- Implement or improve review request automation
- Optimize CTAs and conversion paths
- Improve content engagement (add visuals, examples, better structure)
- Check for technical SEO issues (crawl errors, site speed)

---

### Red Light (Critical Issue)

**Critical Problems:**
- 🚨 Rankings dropping across multiple keywords
- 🚨 Local pack disappearance (was ranking, now not)
- 🚨 GBP suspended or penalized
- 🚨 Manual action in Search Console
- 🚨 Conversion rate <2% at month 6+
- 🚨 Zero reviews in 2+ consecutive months
- 🚨 Negative review with no response

**Immediate Action Required:**
- Full SEO audit (technical, on-page, off-page)
- GBP reinstatement process (if suspended)
- Address manual action (remove violating content/links)
- Landing page optimization urgently needed
- Review generation system broken (fix immediately)
- Reputation management crisis response
