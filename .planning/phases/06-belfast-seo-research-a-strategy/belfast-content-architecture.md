# Belfast Content Architecture

Detailed pillar-cluster content architecture with URL structure, internal linking strategy, and search intent differentiation. Based on belfast-topical-clusters.md.

**Purpose:** Define content structure that delivers topical authority through 4 pillars × 5-8 clusters (25-32 pages) vs competitors' 10-20 pages.

---

## Architecture Overview

### Pillar-Cluster Model

```
[Pillar Page] (2500-3000 words, commercial/transactional)
     ↓ Links to →
[Cluster 1] [Cluster 2] [Cluster 3] [Cluster 4] [Cluster 5] ...
(1000-1500 words each, mixed intent)
     ↑ Link back to pillar
     ↔ Lateral links to related clusters
```

**Benefits:**
- 40% higher visibility vs isolated posts (Semrush 2025)
- Demonstrates expertise through depth, not just claims
- Internal linking distributes authority from pillar to clusters
- Prevents keyword cannibalization through intent differentiation

## URL Structure & Naming Conventions

### Pattern

**Pillar pages:**
- Format: `/uk/{service}-belfast/`
- Examples: `/uk/seo-belfast/`, `/uk/social-media-marketing-belfast/`

**Cluster pages:**
- Format: `/uk/blog/{topic-belfast}/`
- Examples: `/uk/blog/local-seo-belfast/`, `/uk/blog/instagram-marketing-belfast/`

**Service pages (existing):**
- Format: `/uk/services/{service}/`
- Examples: `/uk/services/seo/`, `/uk/services/social-media/`

### Differentiation Strategy

**Avoid cannibalization:**
- `/uk/services/seo/` = Global service offering (6 locales)
- `/uk/seo-belfast/` = Belfast-specific pillar (local focus, Belfast case studies, Belfast pricing)

Different intent:
- Service page: Company-wide service description
- Belfast pillar: Belfast market-specific landing page

## Anchor Text Strategy

### Internal Linking Anchor Text Rules

**From cluster to pillar:**
- Use natural, contextual anchors (NOT exact match)
- Examples:
  - ✅ "our comprehensive SEO services in Belfast"
  - ✅ "partner with Belfast SEO experts"
  - ❌ "SEO Belfast" (exact match = spam signal)

**From pillar to cluster:**
- Descriptive anchor text matching cluster topic
- Examples:
  - ✅ "learn more about local SEO in Belfast"
  - ✅ "read our guide to Instagram marketing"
  - ❌ "click here"

**Lateral cluster links:**
- Topic-relevant anchors
- Examples:
  - ✅ "compare Facebook and Instagram marketing"
  - ✅ "explore our link building strategies"
