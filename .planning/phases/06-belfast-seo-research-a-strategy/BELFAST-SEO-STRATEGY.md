## 5. Content Architecture

### Pillar-Cluster Model Overview

**Architecture Visualization:**

```
[Digital Marketing Belfast Pillar]
        ↓ links to
[SEO Belfast Pillar] [Social Media Pillar] [Google Ads Pillar]
        ↓                    ↓                     ↓
   [6 Clusters]         [5 Clusters]          [5 Clusters]
        ↑                    ↑                     ↑
     Link back            Link back             Link back
```

**Why This Works:**
- 40% higher visibility vs isolated blog posts (Semrush 2025)
- Demonstrates expertise through depth, not just claims
- Internal linking distributes authority from pillar to clusters
- Prevents keyword cannibalization through intent differentiation
- Google recognizes topical authority through content clusters

**Detailed Architecture:** See `belfast-content-architecture.md`

### URL Structure

**Pillar Pages:**
- Format: `/uk/{service}-belfast/`
- Examples: `/uk/seo-belfast/`, `/uk/social-media-marketing-belfast/`
- Purpose: Commercial/transactional landing pages

**Cluster Pages:**
- Format: `/uk/blog/{topic-belfast}/`
- Examples: `/uk/blog/local-seo-belfast/`, `/uk/blog/instagram-marketing-belfast/`
- Purpose: Supporting content, topical depth, long-tail keywords

**Existing Service Pages (No Cannibalization):**
- Format: `/uk/services/{service}/`
- Examples: `/uk/services/seo/`, `/uk/services/social-media/`
- Purpose: Global service description across all 6 locales (US, AU, UK, IE, NL, DK)

**Differentiation Strategy:**
- Service pages = Company-wide service offering (hreflang to 6 locales)
- Belfast pillar pages = Belfast-specific landing pages (local case studies, Belfast pricing, GBP optimization)
- Different search intent prevents cannibalization

### Internal Linking Strategy

**Hierarchy:**
1. Homepage → Pillar pages (high authority distribution)
2. Pillar pages → Cluster pages (authority to supporting content)
3. Cluster pages → Pillar page (authority consolidation)
4. Cluster pages → Related clusters (lateral connections)

**Anchor Text Rules:**

From cluster to pillar:
- ✅ "our comprehensive SEO services in Belfast"
- ✅ "partner with Belfast SEO experts"
- ❌ "SEO Belfast" (exact match = spam signal)

From pillar to cluster:
- ✅ "learn more about local SEO in Belfast"
- ✅ "read our guide to Instagram marketing"
- ❌ "click here"

**Link Volume:**
- Pillar pages: 5-8 links out to clusters
- Cluster pages: 1 link back to pillar, 2-3 lateral cluster links
- Total internal links per page: 6-12 (natural, not spammy)
