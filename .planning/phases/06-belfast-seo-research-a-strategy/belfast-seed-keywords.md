## Rationale by Category

### Core Services (High Priority)
Focus on Rosey Co's main offerings: SEO, social media, Google Ads, web design. These are transactional keywords with highest conversion intent. Target for service pages and pillar content.

### Long-Tail Services (Medium-High Priority)
More specific service variations with lower competition but qualified traffic. Target for cluster content and blog posts.

### Problem-Solving (Medium Priority)
Informational keywords for top-of-funnel content. Lower conversion but builds topical authority and captures early research phase. Target for blog content and guides.

### Comparison (High Commercial Value)
Commercial research intent - users actively evaluating options. High conversion potential. Target for pillar pages and case studies.

### Location Modifiers (Quick Wins)
46% of Belfast searches include "near me" (from 06-RESEARCH.md). Lower competition with GBP optimization. Target for location pages and local content.

### Industry-Specific (Niche Authority)
Target specific verticals to demonstrate expertise. Lower volume but higher conversion and easier to rank. Target for case studies and vertical landing pages.
