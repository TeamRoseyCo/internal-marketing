<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <action>Set up Google Search Console domain property</action>
  <instructions>
This step requires access to DNS and Google Search Console that Claude cannot perform.

**Steps to complete:**

1. **Access Google Search Console**
   - Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Sign in with the account that will manage roseyco.com

2. **Add Domain Property**
   - Click "Add property"
   - Select "Domain" (not URL prefix)
   - Enter: `roseyco.com`
   - Click "Continue"

3. **DNS Verification**
   - Google will provide a TXT record like:
     `google-site-verification=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`
   - Add this TXT record to your DNS:
     - Go to your domain registrar (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, etc.)
     - Find DNS settings for roseyco.com
     - Add new TXT record:
       - Host/Name: @ (or leave blank)
       - Value: the verification string from Google
       - TTL: 3600 or Auto
   - Wait 5-15 minutes for DNS propagation
   - Click "Verify" in Search Console

4. **Submit Sitemap**
   - In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
   - Wait for Google to process (may take hours/days)

**Why Domain Property vs URL Prefix:**
Domain property covers all subdomains, paths, and protocols (http/https) automatically. URL prefix would require creating 6+ separate properties for each locale path.
  </instructions>
