**IMPORTANT:** All templates must be written as genuine human outreach. HARO now detects and bans AI-generated pitches. Templates should provide structure and key talking points but require personalization for each use.

**Document structure:**

## 1. HARO (Help a Reporter Out) Response Templates

**Template A: Belfast/NI Local Business Expert**
- For queries about: local business, marketing in UK regions, SMB strategies
- Positioning: Belfast-based digital marketing agency working with 15,000+ SMBs in Belfast market
- Include: specific Belfast examples (Cathedral Quarter restaurants, Lisburn Road retail, Titanic Quarter businesses)
- Bio section: "[Bailey's name/title], founder of Rosey Co., a Belfast-based digital marketing agency specializing in helping small businesses across Northern Ireland grow through SEO, social media, and paid advertising."

**Template B: Digital Marketing Industry Expert**
- For queries about: SEO trends, social media strategy, paid ads ROI, marketing budgets
- Positioning: Agency with experience across 6 international markets (UK, US, AU, IE, NL, DK)
- Include: concrete metrics (ROAS ranges, cost-per-lead benchmarks, typical SMB results)
- Emphasize practical experience over theory

**Template C: Local SEO & Small Business Growth**
- For queries about: local SEO, Google Business Profile, small business digital presence
- Positioning: Topical authority in Belfast SEO with comprehensive content strategy
- Include: examples of local SEO wins (ranking improvements, GBP optimization, citation strategy)

**HARO best practices section:**
- Only respond to queries where genuine expertise exists
- Respond within 2 hours of query publication for best chances
- Never submit AI-generated responses (HARO detects and bans)
- Include headshot and bio for journalist convenience
- Follow up once if no response after 1 week
- Track pitches sent vs. accepted for conversion optimization

## 2. Belfast Media Pitch Templates

**Template D: Belfast Telegraph Pitch**
- Angle: "Belfast's digital economy growth" / "How Belfast SMBs are competing globally through digital marketing"
- Target: Business section editor
- Format: 150-word pitch email
- Include: newsworthy hook (Belfast SMB digital adoption stats, NI economy trends), offer as expert source for future stories
- Publication URL: https://www.belfasttelegraph.co.uk/

**Template E: Irish News Pitch**
- Angle: "Cross-border digital commerce" / "Belfast businesses reaching Republic of Ireland customers online"
- Target: Business desk
- Format: 150-word pitch email
- Publication URL: https://www.irishnews.com/

**Template F: Belfast News Letter Pitch**
- Angle: "Northern Ireland SMB success stories" / "Digital transformation in Belfast business community"
- Target: Business section
- Format: 150-word pitch email
- Publication URL: https://www.newsletter.co.uk/

**Template G: Belfast Media Group (Community Papers)**
- Angle: Local business spotlight, community business support
- Target: Andersonstown News, North Belfast News, South Belfast News
- Format: 100-word community-focused pitch
- Publication URL: https://belfastmedia.com/

**Media outreach best practices:**
- Research journalist's recent articles before pitching
- Lead with the story angle, not "please link to us"
- Offer genuine value: data, expert quotes, case studies
- Follow Belfast media editorial calendars (business supplements, special editions)
- Pitch on Tuesday-Thursday mornings for best response rates

## 3. Business Partnership Outreach Templates

**Template H: Complementary Business Partnership**
- Target: Belfast businesses offering complementary services (web hosting, business consultancy, print design, coworking spaces, photography)
- Pitch: mutual referral partnership, joint content creation, cross-promotion
- Include: what Rosey Co. brings to partnership (digital marketing expertise, content creation, social media promotion)
- Suggest: guest blog exchange, case study collaboration, referral program

**Template I: Belfast Coworking/Business Hub Partnership**
- Target: Belfast coworking spaces (Ormeau Baths, Blick Studios, Catalyst Belfast)
- Pitch: free marketing workshop for members, digital marketing audit offer, content partnership
- Include: workshop topics Rosey Co. can deliver (Local SEO for Belfast Businesses, Social Media Strategy for SMBs)

**Partnership best practices:**
- Start with giving value before asking for links
- Propose specific collaboration ideas (not vague "let's partner")
- Keep initial ask small (one guest post, one cross-referral)
- Build genuine relationship before requesting reciprocal links

## 4. Community & Sponsorship Templates

**Template J: Charity/Community Sponsorship Enquiry**
- Target: Belfast charities, sports clubs, community organizations
- Pitch: offering digital marketing services (pro bono or discounted) in exchange for sponsor recognition with link
- Examples: Belfast City Marathon charity page, local GAA clubs, community centres

**Template K: Event Sponsorship**
- Target: Belfast business events, networking events, startup events
- Pitch: sponsoring event (providing marketing services, branded materials) in exchange for sponsor page link and logo placement
- Events: Belfast Chamber events, BNI Belfast meetings, Belfast tech meetups

**Community best practices:**
- Genuine community involvement, not just link acquisition
- Start with causes the team genuinely cares about
- Smaller organizations often more willing to feature sponsors prominently
- Document sponsorships for social proof on website

Create a prioritized link building strategy document that serves as the master playbook for Belfast local link acquisition.

**Document structure:**

## Strategy Overview
- Goal: Build locally-relevant link profile for Belfast market dominance
- Principle: Local relevance > Domain Authority (Belfast Chamber DA 45 > generic directory DA 80)
- Timeline: 12-week rolling strategy (Weeks 1-4: Foundation, Weeks 5-8: Outreach, Weeks 9-12: Relationships)
- KPIs: Number of Tier 1 citations live, local links acquired, HARO pitches accepted, domain authority growth

## Priority 1: Foundation Links (Weeks 1-4)
- Complete all Tier 1 citations (reference citation-tracker.md)
- Verify Google Business Profile NAP matches website
- Claim and optimize Foursquare listing (AI search critical)
- Set up HARO account and begin monitoring queries

## Priority 2: Chamber & Organization Links (Weeks 3-6)
**Belfast Chamber of Commerce:**
- Website: https://belfastchamber.com/
- Action: Enquire about membership (cost, benefits, directory listing with backlink)
- Expected link: Member directory page (dofollow TBD - verify before joining)
- Additional benefits: Events, networking, Belfast business credibility

**Northern Ireland Chamber of Commerce:**
- Website: https://www.northernirelandchamber.com/
- Action: Enquire about membership
- Expected link: Member directory at /join-us/our-members/
- Additional benefits: NI-wide business network

**Other organizations:**
- BNI Ireland Belfast chapter (https://www.bni.ie/) - networking + directory
- Digital Marketing Institute Ireland - if marketing-specific directory available
- Invest Northern Ireland business support programs

## Priority 3: Media Links (Weeks 5-8)
**Target publications (in priority order):**
1. Belfast Telegraph (highest readership, DA ~80+)
2. Irish News (major NI daily, DA ~70+)
3. Belfast News Letter (oldest English daily, DA ~60+)
4. Belfast Media Group community papers (hyperlocal reach)

**Approach:**
- Start with HARO to earn media mentions passively
- Pitch 1 story angle per publication per month (not more - avoid being marked as spam)
- Offer expert commentary on Belfast digital economy topics
- Pitch data-driven content (Belfast SMB digital adoption survey, local SEO benchmarks for NI)

## Priority 4: Partnership Links (Weeks 5-12)
**Target partner categories:**
1. Belfast web hosting / IT providers
2. Belfast business consultancies
3. Belfast print/design studios
4. Belfast photography/video studios
5. Belfast coworking spaces (Ormeau Baths, Blick Studios, Catalyst)

**Approach:**
- Identify 3-5 non-competing complementary businesses
- Reach out with specific collaboration proposal (not generic "let's partner")
- Start with guest blog exchange or joint case study
- Build to ongoing referral partnership

## Priority 5: Community Links (Ongoing)
**Sponsorship targets:**
- Belfast City Marathon (charity sponsorship page)
- Local sports clubs (GAA, football, rugby)
- Belfast community centres
- Belfast tech/startup community events

**Approach:**
- Choose 1-2 organizations aligned with team values
- Offer digital marketing services (pro bono or discounted)
- Secure sponsor page listing with backlink
- Document for social proof on website

## Tracking & Measurement
- Track all links acquired in link-tracking spreadsheet
- Monthly link profile review (new links, lost links, link quality)
- Quarterly strategy review (adjust priorities based on results)
- Tools recommended: BrightLocal for citations, Ahrefs/SEMrush for link profile

## Budget Considerations
- Belfast Chamber membership: Enquire (likely GBP 200-500/year)
- NI Chamber membership: Enquire (likely GBP 200-500/year)
- BrightLocal subscription: ~$39/month (citation tracking)
- HARO: Free tier sufficient initially
- Community sponsorships: GBP 50-200 per sponsorship
- **Total estimated: GBP 500-1500 initial + GBP 50-100/month ongoing**
- **Recommendation:** Start with free tools, upgrade after 2-3 clients (per project decision from Phase 6)

## What NOT to Do
- Do NOT buy links from Fiverr or link farm services
- Do NOT submit to 100+ low-quality directories (mass submission is outdated)
- Do NOT use AI to generate HARO responses (detection and ban)
- Do NOT pursue excessive reciprocal link exchanges (A links B, B links A)
- Do NOT chase high-DA generic links over lower-DA Belfast-relevant links
- Do NOT neglect existing citations (maintain and update quarterly)
