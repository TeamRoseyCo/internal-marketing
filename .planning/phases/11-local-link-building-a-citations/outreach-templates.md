### HARO Best Practices

**Response timing:**
- Respond within 2 hours of query publication (highest acceptance rate)
- Queries published 3x daily: morning, afternoon, evening
- Set up email alerts for relevant query categories

**Quality standards:**
- Only respond to queries where you have genuine expertise
- Never submit AI-generated responses (HARO detects and bans)
- Include specific examples, data, and case studies
- Make responses quotable (journalists look for punchy insights)
- Keep responses concise (150-250 words total)

**Follow-up:**
- Follow up once if no response after 1 week
- Don't follow up more than once (journalists are busy)
- Track pitches sent vs. accepted for conversion optimization

**Success metrics:**
- Track: queries responded to, pitches accepted, links earned
- Typical acceptance rate: 10-20% for well-matched queries
- One high-DR media link often worth 10+ directory citations

### Belfast Media Outreach Best Practices

**Preparation:**
- Research journalist's recent articles before pitching
- Follow journalists on Twitter/LinkedIn to understand their beats
- Check editorial calendars for business supplements and special editions
- Save contact information for relevant journalists and editors

**Timing:**
- Pitch Tuesday-Thursday mornings (best response rates)
- Avoid Mondays (busy catching up) and Fridays (planning weekend)
- Allow 1-2 weeks response time before following up
- Don't pitch same publication more than once per month

**Approach:**
- Lead with story angle, not "please feature my business"
- Offer genuine value: data, expert quotes, case studies
- Make it easy for journalist (provide ready-to-use quotes, data, examples)
- Be responsive if they follow up (journalists work on tight deadlines)

**Success metrics:**
- Track: pitches sent, responses received, articles published, links earned
- Expect: 5-10% response rate for cold pitches
- Goal: Establish ongoing relationship as Belfast business expert source

### Partnership Outreach Best Practices

**Preparation:**
- Research potential partners thoroughly (services, audience, values)
- Identify genuine complementary fit (not just any business)
- Prepare specific collaboration proposal (not generic partnership request)
- Understand what value you bring to them

**Approach:**
- Start with giving value before asking for links
- Propose specific, small collaboration first (build trust before bigger asks)
- Emphasize mutual benefit (not one-sided request)
- Build genuine relationship before requesting reciprocal links

**Follow-up:**
- If no response in 1 week, send one brief follow-up
- If still no response, move on (don't be pushy)
- If they agree, deliver excellent value (builds reputation for future partnerships)

**Success metrics:**
- Track: outreach sent, positive responses, partnerships established, links earned
- Expect: 20-30% positive response rate for well-targeted, value-first outreach
- Goal: Build 3-5 ongoing partnership relationships per year

### Community & Sponsorship Best Practices

**Selection:**
- Choose organizations and events aligned with team values (genuine connection)
- Start with 1-2 sponsorships to test approach before scaling
- Prioritize organizations with active online presence (more likely to maintain sponsor links)
- Focus on Belfast-specific organizations for local SEO benefit

**Approach:**
- Lead with value you're offering, not what you want in return
- Be clear about sponsorship recognition expectations upfront
- Don't over-negotiate (these are community organizations, not businesses)
- Follow through on commitments (builds reputation in Belfast community)

**Documentation:**
- Take photos of sponsorship for social proof on website
- Request confirmation of sponsor page link before finalizing agreement
- Track sponsorship ROI (brand awareness, link quality, community goodwill)
- Maintain relationships for potential future sponsorships

**Budget:**
- Typical community sponsorship: £50-200 per organization
- Event sponsorship: £200-500 depending on event size
- Pro bono services: Value at standard rates for tax/accounting purposes
- Set annual community sponsorship budget and stick to it
